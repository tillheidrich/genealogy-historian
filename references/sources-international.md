# International sources

Only resources whose existence and scope are well established are listed. Verify current access before relying on them.

## Prisoners and internees

- **ICRC, Prisoners of the First World War** (grandeguerre.icrc.org): the card index of the International Prisoners of War Agency, about five million names of military prisoners and civilian internees. The index is organised by nationality and phonetic surname groups, not by full-text names: open the drawer for the first letters and page through the cards. Cards list camp, list numbers and often birth date and place; the list references lead to the transmitted lists, also online.
- ICRC archives for the Second World War: not online; requests by family members.
- **Bundesarchiv, Abteilung PA** (Germany): German soldiers and prisoners of war in Allied custody, including records passed on by the United States, Britain, France.
- **Arolsen Archives**: persecution, forced labour, displaced persons, post-war tracing.

## Newspapers

- **Gallica** (Bibliothèque nationale de France): French newspapers with full text, including regional dailies; IIIF image API and ALTO text. Strict rate limits and bot checks; work slowly and let the user pass checks in their browser.
- **Gallica white-label libraries**: regional libraries on the Gallica infrastructure with the same search syntax, for example Yroise (Brest) with La Dépêche de Brest.
- **RetroNews** (BnF, partly paid).
- **Chronicling America** (Library of Congress, United States).
- **Trove** (National Library of Australia).
- **Delpher** (Netherlands).
- **ANNO** (Austrian National Library).
- **Europeana Newspapers** (aggregator).
- **British Newspaper Archive** (paid), **Papers Past** (New Zealand).

## National archives

- **NARA** (United States): military, immigration, census; the online catalogue and the Access to Archival Databases.
- **The National Archives** (United Kingdom): service records, prisoner and internee records, colonial records.
- **Archives nationales** and departmental archives (France): état civil online in most départements, military registers (registres matricules).
- **Archives nationales du monde du travail** (Roubaix, France): records of companies and their staff, for example the Suez Canal Company personnel files; the online index is often by box and name range only.

## Large platforms

- **FamilySearch**: free indexes, catalogue of microfilms; many images restricted.
- **Ancestry, MyHeritage, Findmypast**: paid indexes and images. Use as a finding aid; cite the underlying record, not the platform's tree.
- Online family trees of other users: leads only, never evidence.

## Rules

- Note the language of the record and quote it in the original.
- Check calendar and date formats.
- Record the platform, the collection, the image number and the URL.


## France 1914–1918 (civilian internees)

- ICRC prisoner archive (grandeguerre.icrc.org): cards for civilian internees, often two or more per person with different spellings and dates. Record every card and the conflicts.
- Gallica (BnF) and Yroise (Brest city library) for regional newspapers such as L'Ouest-Éclair and La Dépêche de Brest. Use SRU search and the ALTO text for exact positions. Both throttle (HTTP 429) and Gallica sometimes shows a bot check; wait, or ask the user to open the page.
- Local history literature on camps (for example on the Île de Sieck) often quotes official telegrams and files of the sous-préfecture; trace these to the Archives départementales before relying on them, and treat forum transcriptions as level L.
